One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, written for the plant on the
windowsill. `main.py` writes the fortune — a single line of at most 120
characters — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> Soon the sun will spill across your windowsill, and your keeper will
> brag about you to every guest who walks in.

Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s
[Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
bounty. Standard library only; no third-party dependencies.
